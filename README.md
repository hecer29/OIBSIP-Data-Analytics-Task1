# OIBSIP - Task 1: EDA on Retail Sales Data

## Project Overview

This project focuses on performing Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a retail sales dataset as part of the Oasis Infobyte AICTE OIB-SIP Internship.

The main objective is to explore sales trends, customer demographics, product performance, and relationships between numerical variables.

## Objectives

- Inspect and clean the retail sales dataset
- Analyze descriptive statistics
- Identify monthly and quarterly sales trends
- Analyze customer age groups and gender distribution
- Identify the top 10 best-selling products
- Analyze revenue by product category
- Examine correlations between numerical variables
- Discover additional patterns and insights
- Provide actionable business recommendations

## Technologies Used

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Google Colab / Jupyter Notebook

## Analysis Performed

### Sales Analysis
Monthly and quarterly sales trends were analyzed using line charts.

### Customer Demographics
Customer age groups and gender distribution were analyzed to understand the customer base.

### Product Analysis
The top 10 best-selling products were identified, and revenue was analyzed across different product categories.

### Correlation Analysis
A correlation heatmap was created to examine relationships between numerical variables.

### Additional Analysis
Revenue was analyzed across different age groups to identify additional customer behaviour patterns.

## Key Findings

- The highest monthly sales were recorded in December 2023, while the lowest sales were recorded in December 2024.
- The highest quarterly sales were recorded in Q4 2023, while the lowest were recorded in Q4 2024.
- Customers aged 36–50 represent the largest age group.
- Male customers represent 50.8% of the customer base, while female customers represent 49.2%.
- Azərsun Günəbaxan Yağı 1L was the best-selling product, with 727 units sold.
- Meat generated the highest category revenue at 1922.66.
- Bakery generated the lowest category revenue at 177.07.
- Discount showed very weak correlations with both Sales and Quantity.

## Business Recommendations

1. Maintain sufficient stock for high-demand products and promote similar products.
2. Strengthen strategies for high-revenue categories while investigating opportunities to improve lower-performing categories.
3. Develop targeted marketing strategies for the 36–50 age group.
4. Introduce targeted promotions to increase engagement among younger customers.
